Tee it up.

Drag from the ball to aim. The longer the pull, the more power. Land on the flag-side green and roll into the hole.

🏌️ How to play

Touch and drag backward from the ball, then release to swing. Use shot power to control distance. The ball has gravity, bounce, and sticky landing surfaces. Reach the flag and hole in as few strokes as possible.
