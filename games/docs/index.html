🧠 THE RO'LYFE OPERATING MODEL

RO'Lyfe is not just a collection of links. It is being organized around a simple question:

What opportunity exists — and what system can turn it into an executable business?

🔎 FIND Identify the opportunity.
🧠 ANALYZE Understand the economics.
⚙️ SYSTEMIZE Build the workflow.
💰 FUND Connect capital.
🚀 EXECUTE Turn intelligence into action.

🤖 Humans Need Not Apply — 2026 Edition

RO'Lyfe's automation intelligence framework: don't simply ask which jobs disappear. Analyze which tasks become machine-performed, where capital moves, and where new businesses emerge.

🎯 THE CORE QUESTION

Don't ask which jobs robots will take. Ask which machines will perform the work, who will own them, who will finance them, and who will build the business around them.

🧠 AI RISK How much of the cognitive work can software perform?
🤖 ROBOTICS RISK How much physical work can machines perform?
⚙️ AUTOMATION Can the complete workflow be systemized?
💵 CAPITAL What does it cost to deploy the machine or system?
